# Audience lenses: one product, distinct conversations

These are neutral relevance examples, **not endorsements, customer deployments,
instrument integrations or measured ROI**. The product and its evidence do not change
to fit the audience.

## Pharma discovery: Merck & Co. / MSD

"A screening team can trace an apparent phenotypic response from a compound/dose result
to the source images, see technical-well variability and questionable acquisitions,
and record what should go into confirmatory research."

Use B11 versus F11 to distinguish an interpretable simulated reporter response from
an acquisition-confounded signal. Emphasize independent biological replication and
orthogonal assays, not hit-to-drug claims. Useful value: less manual evidence retrieval,
consistent review context and preserved rationale for repeat work.

This narrative refers to **Merck & Co., Inc., known as MSD outside the United States and
Canada**, not Merck KGaA. Primary context:
[Merck & Co. research](https://www.merck.com/research/).
Do not imply this demo has been used by that company.

## Lab and imaging: Thermo Fisher Scientific relevance

"High-content and 3D workflows need consistent interpretation across fields, runs and
acquisition conditions. A reviewer can inspect image-derived focus/saturation checks
and understand when another acquisition is more useful than another response chart."

Show channel settings, physical spacing, the volume cutaway, linked control comparison
and cross-run QC. The useful conversation is about open export, traceability and
repeatable interpretation, not a claim to replace an instrument vendor's validated tools.

Primary context: [high-content screening and analysis](https://www.thermofisher.com/us/en/home/life-science/cell-analysis/cellular-imaging/hcs-hca.html).
Synthetic instruments are explicitly fictional. The selected public sources identify
PerkinElmer Phenix and Zeiss, respectively; **neither is relabeled Thermo hardware**.

## Separate narrative: Merck KGaA / MilliporeSigma

"For life-science tools and 3D cell-model workflows, a team can keep images, preparation/
acquisition context and interpretation together while comparing experimental consistency."

Use the exact same workbench, but focus the discussion on cell-line-derived spheroids,
material/assay context, image-export contracts and reproducible analysis. Do not conflate
Merck KGaA/MilliporeSigma with Merck & Co./MSD, imply a partnership, or describe every
3D cell culture as an organoid. No reagent/product validation is provided by this demo.

## Scientific informatics / enterprise IT

"The deployment keeps canonical images and derived measurements in tenant-owned storage,
separates analytical tables from transactional reviews, and makes authorization and
source provenance part of the application boundary."

Show the shared schema, exact source manifests, fixed SQL queries, per-user review policy,
fail-closed connection screen and explicit plan/apply/smoke stages. Explain that local
mode is deliberately not the managed Rayfin backend. Minimum app-view access is not
workspace Contributor. Real cloud checks still need the authorized tenant.

## Value without invented ROI

Expected workflow benefits are easier evidence retrieval, faster recognition of
questionable acquisitions, consistent reusable measurements and less manual assembly
of images/metadata/spreadsheets. These are intended benefits, not measured customer savings.
No value calculator, avoided-trial estimate or guaranteed R&D outcome is included.
Actual local loading/interaction timings are reported separately in [verification](verification.md).
